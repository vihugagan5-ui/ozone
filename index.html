Mr. Md. Khan Sab

Experience That Builds Champions

For over 25 years, Mr. Khan has dedicated his life to transforming bodies, improving health, and inspiring confidence in every person who walks onto his floor. What started as his own competitive bodybuilding journey grew into a mission: to build a gym where coaching isn't shouted from the sidelines, but lived out rep by rep, alongside every single client.

Unlike trainers who simply instruct from behind a clipboard, Mr. Khan trains alongside every client — correcting form in real time, adjusting programs on the fly, and holding members accountable the same way he once held himself accountable on the competition stage. That hands-on philosophy is the reason members who "never stuck with a gym before" end up staying for years at Ozone.

A USA Advanced Certified Fitness Trainer (Level 1, 2 & 3 — Anatomy & Physiology) and a proud member of the AP Body Building Association, Hyderabad, Mr. Khan combines internationally recognised education with decades of hands-on floor experience. He holds more than 15 certifications spanning strength training, nutrition, and functional conditioning — knowledge he applies daily to build diet and training plans around each member's real medical history, not a one-size-fits-all template.

Beyond the gym floor, Mr. Khan has represented Andhra Pradesh at the state and national level, competing and placing at bodybuilding championships that shaped the discipline he now instills in every member. At 48, he still trains every day — proof, he says, that the same principles he coaches actually work over a lifetime, not just for a few months.

House Rules

Gym Etiquette

What we expect from every member on the floor.

Hygiene & Health

  • Towel mandatory — no towel, no workout.
  • Wipe down machines after every use.
  • Separate clean indoor shoes required.
  • Members with diabetes, PCOS or heart conditions must keep trainers updated.

Equipment Etiquette

  • Re-rack every dumbbell and plate.
  • No dropping or slamming weights.
  • Share the space; don't sit on machines between sets.
  • Report loose or broken machines to a trainer immediately.

Professional Conduct

  • Advanced lifts only under certified trainer supervision.
  • Proper athletic attire required at all times.
  • Respect the privacy of other members.
  • Keep phone calls short and quiet.

Safety First

  • Warm up for at least 5–10 minutes before every session.
  • Always ask a trainer for a spot when lifting heavy.
  • Stay hydrated.
  • No workout without admission and a base health check.
